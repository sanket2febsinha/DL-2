Founded in 1996, Dreamland Trek & Tour is led by a dedicated team of native Ladakhis who blend youthful energy with decades of local expertise. Based in the heart of Leh, we specialize in curating unforgettable adventures across Ladakh, Zanskar, and Changthang. Founded in 1996, Dreamland Trek & Tour is led by a dedicated team of native Ladakhis who blend youthful energy with decades of local expertise. Based in the heart of Leh, we specialize in curating unforgettable adventures across Ladakh, Zanskar, and Changthang.

We are deeply committed to responsible and sustainable tourism—minimizing our environmental footprint while respecting and uplifting our local communities and heritage. Whether you are seeking high-altitude thrills or a spiritual pilgrimage to the ancient centers of Tibetan Buddhism, Ladakh is an unmatched destination, and we are proud to be your trusted local guides.
