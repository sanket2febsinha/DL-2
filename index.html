Ladakh Adventure Outfit

The management of Dreamland Trek & Tour is a authentic mix of youth energy and seasoned mountain expertise. Since 1996, a group of native Ladakhis located in the centre of Leh has been organizing world-class adventure activities around Ladakh, Zanskar, and Changthang.

The company firmly champions responsible and sustainable tourism, dedicated to reducing environmental footprints while respecting and empowering indigenous villagers and their remote Himalayan communities.
